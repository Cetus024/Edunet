Section 01 · Getting started

Create your account.

Go to edunet-two.vercel.app → Get Started

  1. Open the EduNets home page

    You'll see the headline "You knew Cell Division in March. Do you still?" — that's the right place. Click Get Started in the top-right (or in the middle of the page).

  2. Optional referral code

    If a friend gave you a referral code, paste it in before continuing.

  3. Create your account

    Click Sign up with Google, choose a verified Google account, and continue straight into setup. Already registered? Use Log In instead.

Section 02 · Onboarding

Set up your first learning map.

EduNets setup is a four-step flow. Your first quiz creates a measured starting point for the topic you choose.

  1. About you — "I'm a…"

    Choose Student (this guide follows the student path). Teachers use the separate teaching workspace.

  2. School

    Search the Singapore secondary-school list by name and pick yours. Alternatives (including "prefer not to say") are at the end of the list.

  3. First topic

    Select one subject (e.g. A-Math) and one topic (e.g. Differentiation). This is the first node EduNets places on your learning map.

  4. Starting-point quiz

    Answer all 10 multiple-choice questions from the EduNets database. Feedback is shown only after your first and final submission; the result sets the topic's starting Memory Score.

✦

Your setup survives a refresh — choices and unfinished quiz answers stay in this browser tab until you submit, and you can press Back at any step.

Section 03 · Home base

Your Dashboard.

Sidebar → Dashboard

The dashboard is your daily starting point. Everything on it answers one question: what should I review right now?

Study pulse

A personal greeting with the single most useful action of the day — e.g. "Differentiation in A-Math is at 50%. A quick 8-min review will strengthen your memory."

Memory Health by Subject

One card per subject with a score ring. Cards flagged Needs Review fade first — hit Review Now. Untouched subjects show Not Started with a Start Topic button.

Today's Priority Queue

Your topics sorted by urgency — most forgotten first — each with its Memory Score and an estimated review time (e.g. ~8 mins). Work from the top down.

Section 04 · Test yourself

Take a Smart Quiz.

Sidebar → Smart Quiz

  1. Set up your quiz

    In Quiz Setup, select a subject and topic. The panel shows your Current Memory Score for that topic so you know where you stand before you start.

  2. Choose a quiz mode

    Past Paper (full paper), Concept (up to 5 database concept questions — the default), or Speed (5 random quick-fire questions). Difficulty is set automatically from your Memory Score.

  3. Generate and answer

    Click Generate Concept Quiz. Each question has a 60-second timer. Formats vary — multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, and structured questions.

  4. Learn from every answer

    Feedback is instant: correct answers turn green, wrong ones red, with a short explanation ("Not quite right — a constant does not change as x changes, so its rate of change is zero") and a chip showing which concept it connects to. Click Next Question to continue.

  5. Read your results

    The Quiz Completed screen shows your score, the change vs your last attempt, a breakdown by question type (MCQ / Fill Blank / Structured), and your next review date, scheduled by spaced repetition. Finish with Retake Quiz or View in Concept Web.

✦

Scores move both ways. A rough quiz can drop a topic from 50% to 28% — that's the system being honest with you, and the topic will jump to the top of your Priority Queue with an easier difficulty next time.

Section 05 · See the connections

Explore your Concept Web.

Sidebar → Concept Web → choose a subject

The Concept Web is your subject drawn as a map: every topic is a bubble, connected by solid branch links and dashed cross-topic links. Bubble colour = memory strength.

  1. Pick a subject

    Use the subject dropdown at the top (e.g. A-Math). The subject sits at the centre; topics and sub-topics branch outwards.

  2. Move around

    Click any bubble to open that topic. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. The legend in the corner explains colours and link styles.

  3. Focus on weak spots

    Toggle "Show weak topics only" (top-right) to hide everything that's healthy and isolate what needs work.

  4. Find your friends

    Use "Find your friend" to see squad members' initials pinned to the topics they're working on — useful for choosing what to study together.

Section 06 · Study together

Study Squad.

Sidebar → Study Squad

Leaderboard & streaks

See your squad ranked by overall Memory Score, each with a day-streak counter. Tap a member to expand their per-subject breakdown.

Invite + referral

Invite friends by email, or copy your referral code (e.g. EDUNETS-5DAY) and share it — they enter it when signing up.

Where your squad struggles

Topics flagged At risk show which friend scored low (e.g. "Cell Division — Ben scored 31%"). That's your cue to help.

Send a rescue

Click Rescue [name] to nudge a friend, or Open Rescue Room to start a live 10-minute group quiz on their weak topic.

Section 07 · Live group quizzes

Rescue Rooms.

Study Squad → Open Rescue Room — or open a join link a friend sent you

  1. Join the room

    From a join link you'll land on the Rescue Sprint page: enter your name, pick an initials avatar with the arrows, and click Join Rescue.

  2. Play the rounds

    A rescue has 3 rounds on the struggling topic. Read the question in the centre, type or pick your answer in the Answer panel on the right, and click Submit before the round timer hits zero — everyone advances together.

  3. Watch the rank

    The live Rank panel shows everyone's points, room-only. The room re-syncs every 2.5 seconds, so scores update almost live.

  4. Invite more of the squad

    Click Invite Squad inside the room, tick members who haven't joined, and hit Send selected invites — each invite carries the room's join link.

✦

Completing a rescue counts. Finished rescues credit your squad's group streak — and can restore a streak that was about to break.

Section 08 · Get help

Ask Teacher.

Sidebar → Ask Teacher → + New enquiry

  1. Start an enquiry

    Click + New enquiry, choose a subject, and pick an available Teacher.

  2. Ask your question

    Type your question (up to 4,000 characters) and send it — Ctrl/Cmd + Enter works too. You'll see "Enquiry sent" when it's delivered.

  3. Keep every reply in one place

    All conversations live in Your enquiries on the left, searchable and tagged by subject and topic. Open one to continue the thread anytime — replies also appear as alerts on your Dashboard.

Section 09 · Bring your materials

Capture Hub.

Sidebar → Capture Hub

Feed your own study materials into EduNets — four ways in, one library out.

Live Audio Transcription

Tap the microphone to record a lecture or Zoom class, or import directly from a Teams meeting recording.

Scan Handwritten Notes

Photograph or scan your handwritten notes — OCR extracts the text automatically.

Upload Document

Drag & drop or click to upload PDF, Word, PowerPoint or OneNote files — or Import from OneDrive.

Paste Text

Paste any notes or excerpts into the text box and hit Capture Text.

✦

My Materials Library at the bottom of the page keeps everything you've captured — choose a subject card to filter, or view everything at once.

Section 10 · How scoring works

Understanding your Memory Score.

Every topic has a Memory Score from 0–100%. It rises when you quiz well and decays over time when you don't review — modelling how memory actually fades. The colours below appear everywhere: dashboard rings, concept-web bubbles, and squad pages.

Not Startedno score yet
0–39%Weak — review now
40–69%Needs review soon
70%+Strong — keep it up
What you doWhat happens to the score
Finish the 10-question placement quizGets a starting score from your server-graded result
Score well on a quizScore rises; next review is scheduled further out
Score poorly on a quizScore drops; topic jumps up the Priority Queue with easier questions next time
Don't review for a whileScore decays day by day until you review again
Complete a rescue with your squadBoosts the rescued topic and credits the group streak

Your guide is always around

Spidey, the EduNets mascot in the corner of every page, pops up with tips while you work — "Focus on one question at a time. You have got this." Minimise or close the guide bubble anytime; it'll be back when it has something useful to say.
